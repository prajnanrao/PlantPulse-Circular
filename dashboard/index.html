Circular Digital Twin Mechanics
  • Closed-Loop Water Balance: Captures rainfall across roof area (12 m²) at 80% collection efficiency into a 500L translucent cistern. The smart booster pump prioritizes rainwater before drawing freshwater.
  • Aerobic Biomass Loop: Organic pruning waste (0.35 kg/step) is diverted from landfills into an aerobic bioreactor, undergoing thermophilic breakdown into rich organic compost soil amendment.
  • Machine Learning Optimization: A Random Forest Regressor predicts precise per-bed water needs from soil moisture, temperature, sunlight, and crop factor, avoiding the 6.5L average error of fixed timers.
  • LCA Carbon Accounting: Estimates avoided municipal pumping emissions (0.3g CO₂/L) and chemical fertilizer substitution offset (1.2 kg CO₂/kg compost).
